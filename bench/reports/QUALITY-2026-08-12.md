# Output-quality benchmark — blind, context-free judge

Companion to the speed report ([`ollama-perf-2026-08-12.md`](./ollama-perf-2026-08-12.md))
and findings ([`FINDINGS-2026-08-12.md`](./FINDINGS-2026-08-12.md)). This measures **how
good the outputs actually are**, not how fast.

## Method
- Each model answered the **same 6 French-teaching tasks** (grammar explanation, A2
  writing feedback as JSON, dictionary enrichment as JSON, a B1 subjunctive drill, an
  oral-examiner turn, an EN→FR correction). **Greedy decoding + fixed seed**, so
  differences are capability, not sampling luck. Several tasks have a **known
  ground-truth answer key** (the 4 planted errors in the writing text; *grenouille* =
  feminine; subjunctive "fasses"; present-tense "j'apprends … depuis").
- Outputs were **anonymized** to Model A–F and scored by a **fresh judge agent with
  zero project context** and no knowledge of which model was which — an
  expert-FLE-examiner persona rating accuracy / task-completion / pedagogy / language
  quality, 0–10 per task. Raw scores: [`../results/quality_scores.json`](../results/quality_scores.json);
  captured outputs: [`../results/samples/`](../results/samples/).

## What the judge found (high-confidence takeaways)
1. **14B (q3) is clearly the best quality (8.0).** The only system reliably accurate
   across *every* task: correct grammar with proper present-tense *depuis*, caught all
   4 planted writing errors, right *grenouille* gender, clean subjunctive drill and
   translation. It's also the one that **spills to CPU and runs at ~9 tok/s** (see speed
   report) — so it's a quality-for-latency trade, not a free win.
2. **All 7B qwen quants beat the current llama3.1** (6.5 / 6.33 / 5.67 vs **5.33**).
   llama3.1 was one of the *weakest*: it **fabricated** nearly every writing-feedback
   error (claimed "faisait"→"était", "bu"→"buvais") and used passé composé with
   *depuis*. Switching off llama3.1 is a quality upgrade, not just a footprint one.
3. **The 3B is the worst (3.67)** — and fails exactly where it matters: **malformed
   JSON** on both structured tasks, wrong *grenouille* gender, wrong translation tense,
   a muddled drill. Its speed (43 tok/s) comes at a real quality cost on graded/JSON work.
4. **Quant level (q4/q5/q6) is within noise.** 6.5 / 6.33 / 5.67 across one sample per
   task and one judge is a statistical tie — consistent with the speed finding that
   higher K-quants don't buy quality at 7B. **q4_K_M is the right 7B pick** (tied
   quality, fastest, smallest); q6 is dominated (slower *and* it scored lowest of the
   three here).
5. **A specific 7B weakness to know:** on strict-JSON profiles the 7B qwens left the
   `errors` array **empty** and put the (correct) analysis in the prose `feedback`
   field, and they got the noun **gender wrong** (masculine/null instead of feminine).
   The app's `writing_feedback` and `vocab_enrich` profiles depend on that schema, so
   expect to lean on the offline gender table and to prompt/validate the errors array.

## Combined verdict (speed × quality)

| Model | Quality | Speed (tok/s) | Fit | Verdict |
|---|--:|--:|---|---|
| `qwen2.5:7b-instruct-q4_K_M` | 6.33 | 26.1 | 100% GPU | **Best default** — beats llama3.1 on quality, same speed, smaller |
| `qwen2.5:7b-instruct-q5_K_M` | 6.5 | 25.3 | 100% GPU | Fine alt — ~tied quality, marginally slower/bigger |
| `qwen2.5:14b-instruct-q3_K_M` | **8.0** | 8.9 | ⚠️ spills | **Async/graded only** — best quality, but 47 s answers |
| `llama3.1:latest` (current) | 5.33 | 25.5 | 100% GPU | Incumbent — dominated by 7B qwen |
| `qwen2.5:7b-instruct-q6_K` | 5.67 | 20.9 | 100% GPU | Skip — slower and no quality gain |
| `qwen2.5:3b-instruct-q4_K_M` | 3.67 | 43.2 | 100% GPU | Fast but weak — only for forgiving live turns, not JSON/graded |

### Recommendation (revised, now quality-aware)
- **Default → `qwen2.5:7b-instruct-q4_K_M`.** Best balance: clearly better quality than
  the current llama3.1, identical speed, 100% on GPU, ~1 GB VRAM headroom. (q5_K_M is an
  equally fine pick if you'd rather nudge quality for a hair less speed.)
- **Drop the 3B idea for graded/interactive tutoring.** Its JSON and grammar failures
  outweigh the speed. If you still want a fast lane, restrict it to non-graded chat only.
- **Consider 14B-q3 as an *async* quality tier.** For batch/overnight graded writing
  feedback where 47 s/answer is acceptable, it's a real quality step up (8.0 vs 6.3).
  Not for live use (spills to CPU). Would require per-profile routing, not the single
  global `OLLAMA_MODEL`.

## Caveats
One sample per task and a single (strong) judge — good for ranking tiers, not for
splitting hairs between the 7B quants. Reproduce: `bash bench/gen_on_box.sh` →
`python3 bench/build_eval_packet.py` → judge the packet.
