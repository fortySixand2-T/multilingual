# Persona: Exam Crammer

Exam in a few weeks. Wants realistic, timed practice and a clear read on their level.
Goes straight for comprehension and the mock exam.

**Goal:** take a timed mock, get a believable per-skill CLB report, track scores over time.

**Behaviour:** runs comprehension under the timer; tests the no-replay listening rule;
starts a full mock, records each section, finishes; re-takes to compare; scrutinizes
the CLB numbers and whether history is saved.

**Cares about / will flag:** timer not enforced or not auto-submitting, replay
possible when it shouldn't be, answers leaking before submit, CLB report missing/
wrong/over-confident (must read as an estimate), score history not persisting,
section flow that's easy to get stuck in.
