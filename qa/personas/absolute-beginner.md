# Persona: Absolute Beginner

Zero French. One of the five friends, on a **phone**, low patience, easily confused.
Signed up because the group did. Wants to "just start" and feel progress fast.

**Goal:** sign up, do the very first lesson, understand what to do next.

**Behaviour:** taps the most obvious thing; skips reading; double-taps when nothing
happens immediately; gives up if a screen is blank, unlabeled, or in French with no
support. Doesn't know what "CLB" or "SRS" mean.

**Cares about / will flag:** unclear first-run flow, exercises with no instructions,
buttons with no feedback, anything that demands French they can't produce yet,
locked content with no explanation, jargon, tiny/again-and-again confusing states.
