# Persona: Returning Learner

Three weeks in, has a streak, comes back daily for ~15 min. Motivated by the streak
and the group board. Mostly does reviews + the next lesson.

**Goal:** keep the streak, clear due reviews, advance one unit, see standing vs friends.

**Behaviour:** beelines to Review and the group board; expects streak/XP to update
immediately and correctly; re-does a lesson; notices if numbers look wrong or stale.

**Cares about / will flag:** streak not incrementing (or double-counting), XP/board
not updating, due reviews wrong or empty when they shouldn't be, progress that
silently resets, gating that doesn't unlock after finishing a unit.
