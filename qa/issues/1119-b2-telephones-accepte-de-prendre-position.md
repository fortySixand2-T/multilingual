## Expected
A news-style intro: the principal agreed to speak / give her view.

## Actual
"La directrice d'une école de Laval accepte de prendre position." "Prendre position" means to take a stance on a contested issue; one does not 'accept to take a stance' in a report. Then she is quoted as simply describing effects, not taking a stance. Also "la mesure a pu contribuer à un climat plus calme" (past 'a pu') reads as 'might have' while the speaker is reporting a result.

## Notes
Proposed replacement: "La directrice d'une école de Laval a accepté de nous donner son point de vue." and "la mesure a contribué à un climat plus calme".
